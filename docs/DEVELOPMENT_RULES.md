# Ecommerce UI and Development Rules

## Non-negotiable UI rules

- No purple AI-style gradients.
- No pill-shaped buttons.
- No fake reviews, ratings, testimonials, metrics, customer counts, or counters.
- No vague hero text.
- No emoji icons.
- No em dashes.
- No excessive or theatrical scroll animation.
- No AI-generated filler photos or AI-style filler copy.
- No cursor animation or custom cursor.

## Required design qualities

- Use the client's vibrant orange direction for storefront actions and highlights, while keeping the official KitchenBots logo in its original green. Keep text contrast accessible, using darker orange for text and dark text on bright orange buttons.
- Use real product photography, CAD renders, videos, and specifications.
- Use specific, verifiable copy that explains the product, buyer, value, and next action.
- Use rectangular controls with moderate corner radii and visible focus states.
- Use Lucide icons consistently and provide accessible names where required.
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` and provide static fallbacks for heavy media.
- Design mobile-first and verify 360, 768, 1024, and 1440 pixel widths.
- Keep direct-purchase and quote journeys clear without creating two disconnected websites.

## Evidence and verification

- A UI change begins with a documented usability, accessibility, consistency, or performance problem.
- Do not claim improvement without checking the affected viewport and journey.
- Test keyboard navigation, focus order, errors, empty states, loading, retry, and reduced motion.
- Use Lighthouse and automated accessibility checks as supporting evidence, not as the only review.
- Never replace a real content gap with fabricated copy or imagery.
